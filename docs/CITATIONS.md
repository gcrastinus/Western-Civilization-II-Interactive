# Citations and licences

## Assigned course books (not published with this app)

Reilly, Neal and Cameron, Herzog, and Bod remain on the Student Portal. This app does not reproduce those chapters. After a week ends, a review room may discuss them without substituting for the original.

## Primary extracts (public domain)

- Alexis de Tocqueville, *De la démocratie en Amérique* (1835), Introduction. English: Henry Reeve translation (Gutenberg). French: 1835 text.
- Joinville, Villehardouin, Guillaume le Breton, Froissart — cited as rooms, not printed at length here.
- Galileo, *Sidereus Nuncius* (1610); Jones, Third Anniversary Discourse (1786); Pacioli, *Summa* (1494) — discussed and reconstructed, short public-domain wording only.

## Secondary positions (summaries, short fair-use)

Positions are summarized from works in the course folder and the recommended list: Ganshof, Bloch, Brown/Reynolds, Southern, Chenu, Lewis (frontier; courtly love), Postan, Post, Tierney, Black, Caferro *Contesting the Renaissance*, Dixon *Contesting the Reformation*, Bouyer *The Spirit and Forms of Protestantism*, Kuhn, Shapin, Weber, Tawney, Braudel, Polanyi, Israel, Greene, Hanson, Savigny, Portalis. Quoted blocks are kept short; otherwise the card is a position-summary.
